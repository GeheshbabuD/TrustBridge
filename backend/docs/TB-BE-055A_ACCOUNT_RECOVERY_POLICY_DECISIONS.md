## Current controls that remain mandatory

- Do not expose the existing UUID-based request service directly or accept a client assertion as proof of identity, role, approval, or assurance.
- Keep identity verification server-authoritative and fail closed when its approved authority is absent or unavailable.
- Preserve two distinct active MFA-enabled ADMIN approvals, subject self-approval denial, request expiry, replay/duplicate prevention, audit, session revocation and `authVersion` advancement.
- Keep recovery-code assurance distinct from `TOTP_VERIFIED`; use only server-derived, persisted session assurance.
- Keep existing legacy ADMIN onboarding separate from ordinary USER account recovery.

## Blocking work and next gate

TB-BE-055 ordinary recovery HTTP implementation remains blocked on AR-1 through AR-8, especially a non-enumerating account lookup contract, approved identity authority/reference lifecycle, secure delivery, and the completion TOTP policy. After the responsible stakeholders record approvals, engineering can produce the exact endpoint contract and implement the smallest route/controller/validator/service integration with focused HTTP and isolated PostgreSQL verification. No route, provider, or assurance policy is approved by this register.
