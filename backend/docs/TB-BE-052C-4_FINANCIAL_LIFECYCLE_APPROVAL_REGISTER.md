## Explicitly approved policy that remains in force

The existing reconciliation policy approval remains limited to: eligibility by relevant financial activity regardless of transaction state; a distinct captured-but-not-funded category; 100 default/500 maximum batch size; ADMIN-only manual runs with explicit ADMIN continuation; step-up before detailed findings using server-side authorization; and read-only reconciliation. These decisions do **not** approve the funding trigger, accounting recognition, provider settlement semantics, custody, retention, or detailed-finding purpose configuration.

## Dependency summary

The immediate implementation blocker is the unresolved funding trigger plus the absence of a confirmed, account-specific settlement-evidence contract and approved accounting recognition policy. The following work must remain gated until its prerequisites are approved:

1. A settlement evidence/allocation model and provider-neutral ingestion contract (V-1, V-2, S-1).
2. Decoupling capture persistence from `recordVerifiedFunding` without stranding captured payments (P-1, P-2, P-3, F-1, V-1).
3. Settlement-evidenced escrow journal posting and release eligibility (F-1, F-2, L-1, V-2, R-1).
4. Refund, reversal, chargeback compensation and exception handling (F-2, F-3, L-1, V-2).
5. Historical compatibility and any rollout plan (H-1).
6. Detailed financial exception access and human resolution (S-2, S-3).
7. The affected TB-BE-052C reconciliation classifications can be specified at policy level, but cannot claim settlement confirmation until the settlement evidence model and matching rules exist (V-1, V-2, F-1, S-1).

No database migration, application work, test execution, provider operation, or approval is performed by this register. All rows remain **PENDING APPROVAL** until the required written evidence is attached or referenced and reviewed by the responsible stakeholders.
